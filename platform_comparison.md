# Platform Comparison: Marketplace vs Meta Ads vs Google Ads

> This document covers Section 2.4 and the platform-level findings from Section 5 of the research paper.

---

## Overview

Quick commerce gadget advertising operates across three distinct platform types. Each has a different role in the consumer trust journey, different audience intent levels, and different trust mechanisms.

| Platform Type | Primary Role | Consumer Intent | Trust Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace-native | Purchase conversion | Highest (active shopping) | Seller verification, platform guarantee |
| Google Ads | Discovery + conversion | High (search intent) | Source credibility, Shopping format |
| Meta Ads | Awareness + retargeting | Medium (social browsing) | Social proof, personalization |

---

## Marketplace-Native Advertising

### What it includes
- Sponsored product listings
- Search-based promotion units
- Category banner placements
- Flash sale highlights

### Why it performs best for purchase intent
A user searching for "wireless earphones" on Blinkit is actively looking to buy. This purchase-intent alignment means marketplace ads have the highest baseline CTR and conversion rates of any format.

**Grocery CTR:** 4.2–6.8% | **Gadget CTR:** 1.4–2.7%  
**Grocery Conv. Rate:** 8.5–12.3% | **Gadget Conv. Rate:** 2.1–4.6%

### The transparency problem
Marketplace-native ads are the most variable in disclosure practices. Some platforms clearly label sponsored listings; others use design patterns that consumers mistake for organic search results. This is a trust risk — when consumers later realize a result was paid, it damages platform credibility.

**Policy implication:** All marketplace-native gadget ads should carry clear "Sponsored" labels. Research (Boerman et al., 2017) shows this increases, not decreases, consumer trust.

### Best trust signals for marketplace ads
1. Platform Purchase Guarantee Badge (+44% conversion)
2. Verified Seller Badge (+41% conversion)
3. Brand Authorization Certificate (+33% conversion)

## Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

### What it includes
- Facebook Feed
- Instagram Feed
- Instagram Stories & Reels
- Meta Dynamic Product Ads (DPA)
- Meta Audience Network

### Formats compared for gadget advertising

| Meta Format | Gadget CTR | Gadget Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Static Feed Ads | 0.7–1.4% | 0.9–2.0% |
| Dynamic Product Ads (DPA) | 1.6–2.9% | 2.2–4.1% |
| Stories/Reels | 0.6–1.2% | 0.7–1.6% |

### The DPA advantage
Meta Dynamic Product Ads — which show products based on a user's prior browsing behavior — outperform static formats by **+110% CTR and +92% conversion rate** for gadgets.

This confirms Hypothesis H2: behavioral personalization significantly builds trust in gadget advertising. When a consumer sees a gadget they already browsed, the ad feels relevant, not random. Relevance signals competence — the platform "knows" what they want.

### Meta's role: Social proof and retargeting
Meta Ads are most effective at the middle and bottom of the trust funnel:
- **Mid-funnel:** Video and carousel formats for product education (builds familiarity)
- **Bottom-funnel:** DPA retargeting for consumers who visited the product page but didn't buy

For gadgets, Meta is rarely the first touchpoint — but it is often the re-engagement touchpoint that brings a hesitant consumer back.

### Best trust signals for Meta ads
1. Clear Return Policy in Ad Copy (+38% conversion)
2. Star Rating Display 4.0+ (+29% conversion)
3. Transparent Pricing (+36% conversion)

## Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Display)

### What it includes
- Google Search Ads
- Google Shopping Ads
- Google Display Network (GDN) / Retargeting

### Formats compared for gadget advertising

| Google Format | Gadget CTR | Gadget Conv. Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Search Ads | 2.3–4.1% | 3.4–6.8% |
| Shopping Ads | 2.8–4.7% | 3.9–7.2% |
| Display/GDN Retargeting | 0.5–1.0% | 1.2–2.7% |

### Google Shopping: The trust-optimized format
Google Shopping has the **smallest performance gap between grocery and gadget categories** of all formats tested (1.8× difference vs 4.1× for Meta Stories). This is because Shopping ads bundle multiple trust signals natively:
- Product image
- Final price
- Seller name
- Star ratings

These elements directly address consumer concerns before a single click. The format does trust-building work automatically.

### Google Search: The intent advantage
Google Search ads target consumers actively searching for a gadget — they are already in evaluation or purchase mode. Trust is the final barrier, not the first. This explains why Google Search shows the narrowest grocery-gadget gap among search-intent formats.

**Insight for campaign managers:** For gadgets priced above INR 5,000, Google (2022) found that consumers need an average of **7–8 touchpoints** before purchasing. GDN retargeting is therefore not an optional extra — it is a required part of the trust-building sequence.

### Best trust signals for Google ads
1. Star Rating Display in Shopping format (+31% CTR)
2. Brand Authorization Certificate (+26% CTR)
3. Transparent Pricing (+28% CTR)

## The Multi-Platform Trust Journey

The most effective gadget advertising does not rely on a single platform. The data shows the average gadget buyer interacts with **6.4 ads across multiple platforms** before purchasing (vs 1.8 for grocery).

**Recommended budget allocation for gadget advertising:**

| Platform | Role | Budget Allocation Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search + Shopping | Primary conversion | 40–45% |
| Meta DPA | Retargeting + re-engagement | 25–30% |
| Marketplace-native | High-intent conversion | 20–25% |
| Google GDN | Retargeting support | 10–15% |

Each platform plays a distinct role in the trust journey. Concentrating budget in one channel creates gaps in the trust sequence that competitors can exploit.
