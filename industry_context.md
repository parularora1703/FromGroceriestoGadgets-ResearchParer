# Industry Context: Macro Data Supporting This Research

> This document situates the paper's findings within the broader global and Indian digital advertising landscape, drawing on Dentsu, GroupM, eMarketer, and RedSeer reports.

---

## Why Quick Commerce Advertising Matters — The Scale

### India's Quick Commerce Market
- Estimated market size in 2022: **USD ~1 billion** (RedSeer, 2023)
- Projected market size by 2025: **USD 5.5–7 billion** (10–15× growth)
- Primary driver: Urban millennials and Gen Z prioritizing speed over cost
- Key platforms: Blinkit (Zomato), Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket

### India's Digital Advertising Growth
India is the **fastest-growing digital ad market** among the four largest economies in Asia-Pacific:
- India digital ad spend growth: **+20.2%** in 2025 (Dentsu APAC Report, 2025)
- Growth driven by: paid social, video, and AI-driven ad placements
- India ad spend growth in 2024: **+6.8%** — most dynamic market in APAC (Dentsu, 2024)

## Global Retail Media Trends (Directly Relevant to Quick Commerce)

Retail media — advertising within e-commerce and quick commerce platforms — is the **single fastest-growing segment** of global digital advertising.

| Metric | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Global retail media YoY growth (2024) | +32% | Dentsu Global Ad Spend Forecasts, 2024 |
| Global retail media 3-year CAGR (to 2027) | +21.9% | Dentsu Global Ad Spend Forecasts, 2025 |
| Retail media share of digital ad spend by 2024 | >23% | Forbes / Camphouse, 2024 |
| Global digital ad spend (2024) | $442.6–$772.4 billion | Dentsu (various editions) |

**Why this matters for the paper:** The explosive growth of retail media validates why quick commerce platforms are investing so heavily in advertising infrastructure — and why getting gadget advertising right is commercially critical.

## The Algorithmic Era of Advertising

Dentsu's 2025 Global Ad Spend Forecast identifies a shift to what it calls the **"algorithmic era"** of advertising:
- Algorithmically-enabled ad spend estimated at **59.5% of global ad spend in 2024**
- Projected to reach **79.0% by 2027**
- This directly powers the personalization capabilities (Meta DPA, Google Smart Shopping) that this paper identifies as trust-building tools for gadget advertising

## Consumer Touchpoints and the Multi-Platform Reality

Google (2022) found that consumers need an average of **7–8 touchpoints** before purchasing electronics priced above INR 5,000. This paper's retargeting analysis found the average gadget buyer on quick commerce platforms interacts with **6.4 ads** across platforms before purchasing.

This alignment of academic/industry data with campaign performance data strengthens the paper's core argument: gadget advertising requires a multi-platform, multi-touchpoint trust-building sequence, not a single-channel campaign.

- Digital ad spend global growth (2024): **+12.2%** with programmatic playing a key role (eMarketer, 2024)
- Programmatic channels account for **>70% of digital ad spend** globally
- Programmatic expected to grow at **10.2% 3-year CAGR** to 2027 (Dentsu, 2025)
