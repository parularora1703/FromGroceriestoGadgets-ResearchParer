# From Groceries to Gadgets: Role of In-App Advertising in Shaping Consumer Trust

Authors: Parul Arora · Kartik Bhardwaj  
Institution: Chitkara University, India  

---

## Overview

This research investigates how in-app advertising on quick commerce platforms (like Blinkit and Zepto) affects consumer trust — particularly as these platforms expand from everyday groceries into high-value gadgets and electronics.

The study uses secondary data analysis combined with anonymized retail media campaign performance data to understand what advertising strategies build (or break) consumer trust.

---


## Key Findings at a Glance

| Metric | Grocery Ads | Gadget Ads |
| Marketplace CTR | 4.2–6.8% | 1.4–2.7% |
| Google Search CTR | 4.7–7.2% | 2.3–4.1% |
| Marketplace Conv. Rate | 8.5–12.3% | 2.1–4.6% |
| Avg. touchpoints before purchase | 1.8 | 6.4 |

**Trust Signal Impact (Gadget Ads, A/B tested):**
- Combined trust signals → **+58% CTR, +73% Conversion Rate**
- Platform Guarantee Badge alone → **+44% conversion rate**
- Verified Seller Badge → **+34% CTR**

---

## Research Questions

1. How does consumer trust differ between grocery vs. gadget advertising on quick commerce platforms?
2. Which advertising attributes (transparency, personalization, frequency, credibility) most affect gadget ad trust?
3. How do different platforms (marketplace, Meta, Google) affect purchase intention for gadgets?
4. What role do trust signals play in closing the trust gap in gadget advertising?

---

##  Methodology

- **Design:** Descriptive, non-experimental
- **Data:** Secondary industry reports + anonymized retail media campaign data (Jan 2023 – Dec 2024)
- **KPIs tracked:** CTR, Search Ad Visibility, Retargeting Engagement, Add-to-Cart Rate, Conversion Rate, ROAS, Trust Signal Uplift
- **Platforms:** Marketplace-native ads, Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads (Search/Shopping/Display)

---

## Industry Context

This research is situated within a rapidly growing digital advertising landscape:
- India's quick commerce market projected to grow **10–15x** to USD 5.5–7 billion by 2025 (RedSeer, 2023)
- India leads APAC in digital ad spend growth at **+20.2%** (Dentsu, 2025)
- Retail media is the **fastest-growing** digital ad segment globally at **+21.9% YoY** (Dentsu, 2025)
- Global retail media grew **32% YoY** in 2024, faster than any other digital channel (Dentsu, 2024)
